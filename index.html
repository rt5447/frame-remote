Connect to your TV

Your phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi.

Where do I find the IP address?
  1. On the TV: Settings → General → Network → Network Status → IP Settings (on newer models: Settings → Connection → Network).
  2. Or in your Wi-Fi router's app, listed as "Samsung" or "The Frame".
  3. Tip: in your router, reserve that address for the TV so it never changes.
First-time setup (one-time)
  1. The TV uses a private security certificate. Tap "Approve TV certificate" when this page asks, then choose Advanced → Proceed (Android) or Show Details → visit this website (iPhone). Then come back here.
  2. The TV shows a pop-up asking to allow "Phone Remote". Choose Allow. After that it connects silently.
  3. Add this page to your home screen so it opens like an app.
